```

AND risk_level >= threshold
AND last_alert_for_same_issue > 6h ago
```

* SL missing or failed
* TP missing for > X minutes
* Auto-hedge activated
* Funding regime flip
* Volatility spike detected
* News freeze engaged
* Circuit-breaker fired
* Kill-switch armed
* Exposure exceeds limit
* API degraded or stuck
* Position unprotected
* Repeated execution failure
